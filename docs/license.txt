If you're asking what the "About Me" and "License" pages should look like for the jDroid-X Free Model Club app, I recommend keeping them separate and very clean.

1. About Me / About jDroid-X

I would actually call the page "About jDroid-X" rather than "About Me". It feels more like a product/company page and gives you room to grow.

2. License page

The License page should not simply say "MIT License" unless the entire project is actually released under MIT.

This is particularly important for Free Model Club, because you may have:

jDroid-X source code
third-party libraries
provider APIs
AI models
model metadata
icons
fonts
images
open-source dependencies

Each can have different licenses.

4. Particularly important for Free Model Club

I'd include this disclaimer:

Free Model Club does not guarantee the availability, performance, pricing, token limits, or free usage quotas of third-party AI providers or models. Provider policies, model availability, API limits, and pricing may change without notice. Users are responsible for complying with the terms and policies of each provider they use.

And another important one:

Free Model Club is an independent jDroid-X application and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, NVIDIA, Microsoft, Meta, xAI, or other third-party AI providers unless explicitly stated.

That becomes especially useful because your application may list models from many companies.
